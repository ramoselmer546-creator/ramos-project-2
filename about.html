A Legacy of Faith & Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served as a home of Catholic education in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental.

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Vision: A Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

The Four Pillars

Academic Excellence

Building knowledge, critical thinking, and a love of learning.

Faith Formation

Deepening faith through Catholic tradition and Gospel values.

Community Service

Putting faith into action through meaningful service.

Holistic Development

Developing capable learners with strong character.
